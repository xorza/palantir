# Open issues

- Measure cache: in a Hug stack whose content is taller than its available height, changing one leaf's height leaves zero cache hits in the whole tree on the next frame, though every sibling subtree keeps its subtree hash. `AxisSlot::inner_avail` floors the stack's main available at its own intrinsic min, which moves with any descendant's height, so every child's quantized available key moves with it (seen in a 8 × 8 × 8 tree of 1 px leaves: 512 → 513). The `layout/cache/bench.rs` `broad` variant changes a fill weight instead, so it does not show this.
- Glyph ink outside a text run's advance box — italic overhang, a negative left side bearing — lies outside both the run's damage rect (`src/scene/cascade/paint_rect.rs` `inflate_text_damage`) and its text batch scissor (`src/renderer/frontend/composer/session.rs` `text`); neither reads glyph ink bounds. A run alone is cut at its box plus the scale-step pad, and the same run batched with a wider neighbour shows its overhang.
- A Hug `wrap_vstack` below a 15 px header in a vstack capped at 100 px measures against the whole 100 px, not the 85 px left under the header: two 40 px cells share a column, and the wrap ends at 105 px, past the cap (`layout/wrapstack/tests/bounds.rs`, `wrap_vstack_wraps_against_a_main_bound_wherever_it_lives`, case "cap on an hstack of columns"). `Stack::measure` gives every non-Fill child the stack's full main extent.
- In a FILL zstack (padding 16) holding a Fixed-360 Hug vstack (padding 8) with a 14 px `WrapWithOverflow` paragraph that wraps to three lines, an 800 × 50 surface arranges the paragraph one line tall and the inner panel 33 px, not 67 (`layout/cross_driver_tests/text_wrap/wrapping.rs`, `fill_panel_grows_to_contain_wrapped_content_on_y`). The text still shapes to three lines (51 px); the rects shrink below it. Under a finite available height `AxisSlot::resolve` floors a Hug node at its Y intrinsic min, and `IntrinsicQuery` computes that at unbounded width, where wrapping text is one line — so neither the text leaf nor its panel is floored at the height its text takes at the width it is laid out at.
